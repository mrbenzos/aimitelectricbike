1. Platform Purpose & Overview

The AIMIT Electric Bikes Ghana platform is an e-commerce, test ride booking, and fleet management system built specifically for Ghana. It enables prospective buyers across Accra, Tema, Kumasi, and nationwide to discover authentic heavy-duty electric bicycles, calculate their fuel savings in Cedis, order online or via WhatsApp, and book in-person trials.

Key Client Advantage: The admin console requires zero technical or coding knowledge. Managers can add bikes, update prices, mark items sold out, and review customer bookings through a simple visual dashboard on their laptop or mobile phone.

4. Standard Operating Procedures (Daily Routine)

1
Morning Opening (8:30 AM): Log into aimitelectricbike.com/admin with your 2FA SMS code. Review the Test Rides tab to see who is visiting today. Have the hub technician ensure trial bikes are fully charged.
2
Order Processing: When an SMS alert arrives for a new order, open the Orders tab. Call or WhatsApp the customer to confirm their delivery address or preferred pickup hub.
3
Stock Updates: If a shipment arrives or a showroom model sells out, click the Pencil or Sold Out button in Fleet Management to keep the website 100% accurate.
